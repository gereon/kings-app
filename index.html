Kings

Kings is a logic puzzle for iPhone, iPad and Mac. Put one crown in each row, each column and each color region. Two crowns cannot touch.

Questions

When does a new daily puzzle start?
Every day at 06:00 UTC. That is 08:00 in Central Europe in summer and 07:00 in winter. The app shows the time in your time zone.

Why does my time not show on the leaderboard?
Only the first solve of the daily puzzle counts, and only on its own day. You must be signed in to Game Center. Archive and free play puzzles are not ranked.

Why does the clock not stop?
The daily puzzle is a race. The clock starts when you tap Start and runs until you solve the puzzle, also when you leave the app. This makes the times on the leaderboard fair.

How does a streak freeze work?
For each 7 days in your streak, you get one streak freeze (up to 3). If you miss a day, a freeze keeps your streak alive.

Is my progress saved?
Yes. Your progress, streak and best times sync between your devices with iCloud.
