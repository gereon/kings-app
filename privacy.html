Privacy policy

Last change: 11 October 2026

No data collection

Kings does not collect, store or share any personal data. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The developer has no server for the app.

Apple services

Kings uses two Apple services. Apple handles this data under the Apple Privacy Policy, and the developer cannot see it.

  • Game Center: if you sign in, your solve time of the daily puzzle goes to the Game Center leaderboard with your Game Center name.
  • iCloud: your game progress, streak and best times sync between your devices through your own iCloud account.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, also not from children.

Contact

For questions about this policy, please open an issue on GitHub.
